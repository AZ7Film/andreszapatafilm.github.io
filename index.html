About

"Interpreting vision and translating it into cinematic presence."

With over 25 years behind the lens in New York City, Andrés Zapata has built a career on the kind of instinct that can't be taught — forged through hundreds of productions, every condition imaginable, and an uncompromising eye for light, composition, and human truth.

From institutional documentaries to narrative short films, commercial campaigns to independent cinema, Andrés delivers visual excellence at every scale.
